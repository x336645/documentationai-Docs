---
title: Cannot Access Documentation
description: Fix issues accessing your documentation.
---

## Symptoms

- Docs page not loading
- 404 or access denied errors

## Causes

- Not logged in
- Wrong workspace selected

## Solution
<Steps>
  <Step title="Check login status">
    ````
    Log out and log back in from your dashboard.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify workspace">
    ````
    Ensure you're in the correct workspace. Switch using the workspace menu at the top.
    ````
  </Step>
</Steps>
