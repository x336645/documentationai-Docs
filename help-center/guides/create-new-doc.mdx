## Get Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your Dashboard">
    ````
    Login and click "Dashboard" from the sidebar.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'New Page'">
    ````
    Find the "New Page" button at the top right.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in details">
    ````
    Enter your page title and select a category.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and View">
    ````
    Click "Save" to publish. Your doc appears in the main list immediately.
    ````
  </Step>
</Steps>
