## Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure you have:

- A modern web browser like Chrome, Firefox, or Safari
- An email address for registration
- Access to `https://aitw.biz`

<Callout kind="info">

No prior AI knowledge required. The platform follows the slogan: `零技術門檻，AI技能輕鬆上手` (Zero technical barriers, master AI skills effortlessly).

</Callout>

## Create Your Account

Follow these steps to sign up and log in quickly.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Web Browser" icon="globe">

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the Site" icon="external-link">
    Navigate to [https://aitw.biz/zh](https://aitw.biz/zh).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Sign Up" icon="user-plus">
    Select `登入 / 註冊` and choose the registration option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Details" icon="edit-3">
    Provide your email, create a password, and agree to terms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Email" icon="mail">
    Check your inbox for the verification link and click it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Mobile App" icon="smartphone">

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download App" icon="download">
    Search for Danny Chang AI Academy in your app store.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open and Register" icon="user-plus">
    Tap `Sign Up` and follow the on-screen prompts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm" icon="check-circle">
    Verify via email or SMS code.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Set Up Your Profile

Personalize your experience for better recommendations.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Profile" icon="user">
    Click your profile icon in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update Info" icon="edit">
    Add your name, interests (e.g., `AI for business`, `beginner tutorials`), and learning goals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Changes" icon="save">
    Click `Save` to enable personalized course suggestions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Expandable title="Advanced Profile Options" default-open="false">

Upload a profile picture and connect social accounts for sharing progress.

</Expandable>
